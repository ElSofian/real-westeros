# Personal restitution promises — explicit consent, no forced land transfer

**Original CK3 scripts; static-checked, not engine-tested.** This authored slice
adds a personal undertaking to an existing exact-title restitution discussion. It
connects negotiation, obligations, knowledge, memory and discretionary consequences.
No native grant, legal claim, coercive remedy or global title-ban enforcement is added.

## From discussion to an actual personal commitment

The original captured holder may explicitly offer a free promise to return the
captured ordinary title within thirty days. This requires the exact private request,
live `talks` window and current original source/claimant/ownership conditions. Merely
accepting examination, opening a panel or viewing native grant terms is not a promise.
One offer history per discussion prevents replacing its clock with a fresh offer;
there is no cap on unrelated requests/titles, and counteroffers have no count cap.

The beneficiary responds to thirty days; they may counter with fourteen. The holder
may accept fourteen or counter with thirty. Each counter increments a revision,
changes the responder, and invalidates every older offer panel. Acceptance belongs
only to the current responder of that exact revision/delivery clause. The proposer
already consented to those terms when sending them. New replies are sent after one
day, for AI and humans alike, to avoid an immediate AI counteroffer loop. The
original seven-day offer window and original discussion window both limit consent;
neither is reset by counters, reading or waiting. Either participant may close an
unaccepted offer without penalty even if eligibility/window conditions have changed.

Acceptance consumes the offer before writing the accepted date, promised state and
one fixed 14/30-day due marker. The only hidden timeout is scheduled for day 15/31
before notifications. No payment, escrow, prestige, opinion, title or claim is created.
There is no inference that a native grant is currently legal or guaranteed.

## Accepted promises stay distinct from the conversation

After consent, the promise is personal to the captured parties/title. Its agreed
period starts at acceptance, independently of the older conversation deadline.
Closing/refusing/withdrawing the conversation does not release or renounce this
accepted commitment. The original refusal reaction is disabled for accepted-pledge
records, so merely closing those talks adds no second penalty. A dedicated message
explains the separation and the original private history opens the promise panel.

A later house head, sovereign or title holder inherits no consent. Original public
source expiry or a later house-head change does not itself erase the accepted
personal promise; its private request history must still exist and be retained.
Original requester death ends that private story/index as before. Original holder
death or title loss to a third party can neutrally invalidate a pending promise at
the existing sixty-day reconciliation. Current action guards apply immediately;
unobserved transient title changes/reversals can be missed. No new global index,
periodic timer, native on_action or world/pair scan is introduced.

The beneficiary may freely release a promised/unresolved commitment without present
grant eligibility, funds or current authority. The holder may explicitly renounce
an unexpired accepted promise only while still personally holding that title; this
records the holder's statement, not a native legal judgment. Both terminal actions
consume the due marker before notices. An expired or third-party title cannot be
used to manufacture an explicit renunciation response.

## Native performance and beneficiary confirmation

A human promising holder may open ordinary native grant terms only while the exact
promise is unexpired, both original parties are living adult/free/at peace, the exact
ordinary title is still theirs, and the native interaction is valid for the captured
beneficiary. The click repeats these checks. No target/title, hook or send option
is preselected or auto-sent. The actual title must be selected and confirmed there.
Native special-window eligibility remains experimental; the ordinary native menu
is the fallback. Existing AI native grants are unchanged; there is no forced AI
land-gift selector or claim that AI promises will reliably be performed.

The received source audit locates the real grant in engine code, not a demonstrated
callback proving this discussion caused it (TITLE_ROUTE_AUDIT.md). Therefore current
beneficiary ownership is displayed separately and never automatically fulfills a
promise. If both original parties remain available and the beneficiary currently
holds this exact title, that beneficiary may explicitly confirm satisfaction. This
records a **personal statement plus current ownership**, not proof of a voluntary
grant, a particular acquisition route or causal compliance. No resources or reward
follow. A confirmation after the deadline is labelled late confirmation; it does
not claim late acquisition. No passive observer can write that acknowledgment.

The original requester/tuple/revision/delivery-only timeout runs only after actual
expiry and only from `promised`. It records `unresolved`, not deliberate breach,
nonperformance or a crime. Silence cannot prove when/how title ownership changed.
Existing sixty-day maintenance supplies a neutral fallback if that callback is lost.
The beneficiary may still release the unresolved promise or confirm satisfaction
while actually holding the title. Timing, pointers and same-tick ordering await CK3.

## Optional personal response to an explicit renunciation

Only the original beneficiary can react within seven days of explicit renunciation,
with no earlier response, both original parties currently available and the title
still held by the renouncing holder. They may acknowledge without penalty or choose
personal resentment: **−15 opinion for two years**, only if that pair's same modifier
is absent. It neither stacks nor renews across promises/titles. The response is
consumed before the modifier check/effect; history records the choice without
claiming its modifier lasts forever. Silence, failed offers, unresolved deadlines,
release and neutral invalidation never apply a penalty automatically.

No guilt is inherited and no crown/house successor sees these private replies.
All new gameplay guards/effects are shared by AI and player. The only human-only
predicate opens a native UI shortcut. AI has the same initial discussion option,
versioned reply events, beneficiary confirmation, renunciation/release and reaction
choices; traits influence choices and it avoids offering its primary title. Actual
AI rates, native grant selection and balancing remain unverified.
