# Private explanations and personal reactions after a restraint-role observation

**Original CK3 scripts; static-checked, not engine-tested.** This extends the personal
military-restraint record. A native role observation, a promisor's statement about
intent and a beneficiary's reaction remain distinct. Nothing creates a native
crime, proof of voluntary war initiation, penalty or peace automatically.

Only a **fresh** exact military-role observation opens one thirty-day private window.
The callback first consumes the accepted pledge and writes its observed roles/date;
a same-call record token then binds response schema, serial and observed date before
any initial notice. The token is cleared before notices. Existing schema blocks
reinitialization. Prior observations, expiry, renunciation, release, defensive/secondary
roles, lost callback context and archive reads cannot create or renew this window.
No old records are retrospectively promoted to new consent opportunities.

The original beneficiary may request an explanation **once**, react immediately or
wait. A request records its date before one private promisor notice. If requested,
the original promisor may explicitly acknowledge responsibility for breaking their
undertaking or contest that the observed roles imply a voluntary breach. Either
answer records the chosen statement/date before one beneficiary notice; it is not
an independent finding of truth. An unanswered request stays unanswered, including
when the window expires. Neither asking nor answering extends the original deadline.

During that same window the original beneficiary can once:

- **Resent the event personally:** −15 opinion of the original promisor for two years,
  only if this exact pair modifier is absent. Repeated pledges cannot stack or
  refresh an active modifier. Another existing grievance remains independent.
- **Take note:** record a neutral reaction without adding/removing opinion modifiers,
  forgiving another dispute, erasing the role fact or granting a native pardon.

A reaction is allowed with or without a requested explanation or reply. Personal
judgment is not a legal proof threshold: a contested statement does not force
forgiveness, and an acknowledgment does not force resentment. Either reaction
consumes the window and records state/date **before** optional opinion and one private
promisor notice. A later pending reply can no longer change the closed reaction,
and a replay cannot replace either statement or reaction. Silence/expiry creates
neither opinion, admission, consent nor pardon.

All actions require the original living parties and exact retained typed pledge,
owner, captured parties/serial, schema and observed date. Statement views require
request/answer dates where applicable, terminal reaction views require their date.
No heir, bystander, mediator, common liege, wrong story or stale actor receives
rights. Current money, rank, prison/war capacity need not permit a new pledge simply
to give a personal explanation or react to an already retained fact. Original owner
death/retention behavior remains; no record/offer/due/retention/cooldown is extended.

The existing original-party panel and archive show facts, statement and reaction
separately. All notices stay between original parties. No extra native hook, callback,
world scan, recurring reminder, payment, prestige, secret, imprisonment, title,
truce, CB or war outcome operation is added. The role observer and native wars
remain unchanged apart from same-call response initialization. G1 is still a separate
unresolved authentic-CB/royal-ban feature; this is not its replacement.

AI and player share the same choices, actor/window guards and opinion effect. Existing
fresh notices provide bounded opportunities. Trait weights consider honesty,
deceitfulness, justice, forgiveness and vengefulness; acknowledgment may affect
reaction willingness without becoming truth. Timing, balance, actual AI choices,
privacy/save/load and opinion duration are not demonstrated in CK3.

Fifteen adversarial source tests cover fresh-token ordering/no legacy backfill,
original-party identity/window, one request/reply, explicit answer enum, no automatic
truth or reaction, consume-before-effects, non-stacking opinion, neutral choice,
native-state isolation, private bounded notices, AI parity and EN/FR. These are not
a gameplay simulation. MRESP-01…10 in FINAL_ENGINE_TESTS.md remain unrun, alongside
MREST-01…12. Existing native war API references are reused; no proprietary assets,
additional source upload, paid service, runtime Python/LLM or intermediate game run.
