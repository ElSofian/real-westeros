# Recorded promise clauses in mediated disputes

**Original CK3 scripts, static-checked only; not engine-tested.** This slice connects
negotiated endorsement terms, terminal receipts and existing private case records.
It does not add a service, sanction, payment, native callback or Python gameplay.

## What the participants can read

A newly disclosed service-breach case copies the actual terminal receipt's agreed
price (25/50 gold), original delivery period (14/30 days), deferred endorsement kind,
accepted contract revision and receipt date. The revision identifies original
accepted consent; it is not a version history of subsequent bilateral price
amendments. An accepted 50-to-25 amendment must yield price 25 in the eventual
receipt and case. A proposed, refused or ignored amendment must leave price 50.
The price is consideration payable only at performance, not a debt or payment
already taken. The recorded delivery period starts at original promise acceptance,
not referral or reading. No new due date is created.

Existing mediation, repair, result and case-history panels offer **Read the clauses
recorded for this promise**. A separate read-only panel shows the captured terms;
there is no payment, decision, admission or deadline extension on reading. Both
AI and player cases use the same receipt/copy path. AI does not browse this cosmetic
panel; existing political response choices and their weights stay unchanged. This
is not a new AI reasoning policy or general negotiation composer.

## Source identity and immutability

The terminal-receipt writer first removes any old typed-terms marker, accepted
revision, kind/mode and date. Complete current deferred obligations with an explicit,
matching stored delivery clause and supported terms get schema 1 only after all
fields have been written. The authoritative current contract/quote identity must
match; missing legacy delivery cannot become a certified default. Existing receipt
outcome and breach generation remain authoritative.

Fulfillment, accountable breach and mutual release call that shared writer before
ordinary contract cleanup. The independent original timeout already refreshes the
current agreed price after exact expired-owner checks (RW010_PRICE_AMENDMENTS.md).
Neutral orphan/unavailable cancellation clears typed metadata rather than retaining
a previous receipt's certificate; it does not manufacture a breach or usable terms.

Only explicit fresh service referral calls the copy helper. It rechecks the unconsumed
breach-response opportunity, exact latest receipt serial/client, typed terms and
fresh case type/owner/serial/client/kind. It writes the copied fields before their
schema marker, then referral consumes the original response and sends its notices.
No current offer scalars are copied. Later services can replace the provider's
latest receipt without changing the independent earlier case. No archive callback,
allegation, ruler succession, timer or reader backfills clauses into old cases.

These ordered writes are not a demonstrated atomic CK3 transaction or crash-recovery
protocol. Receipt mutation and event ordering remain final-engine questions.

## Privacy, old records and unchanged consequences

Opening the panel and displaying its clauses both recheck the full existing case
identity and original role/disclosure reader predicate. Only the provider, captured
client and already-informed captured mediator know these terms. Current rank alone
confers no access. An unrelated reader, wrong case kind, obsolete pointer or wrong
identity gets no clauses. Case views use the selected story, never the provider's
current service or latest receipt. Pending/closed known cases can be read without
reviving jurisdiction or handing consent to an heir.

A known service case with no complete typed copy explicitly says the price/delivery
are unavailable. Its existing decision flow remains available where originally
eligible; unknown clauses do not retrospectively cancel it or invent proof. An
allegation is not a service receipt and receives no service clauses. Existing
personal owner-death ending, 1825-day closed retention and periodic cleanup are
unchanged. This is not a complete promise ledger or dynastic archive.

No new gold, prestige, opinion, crime, execution, title transfer, inherited guilt,
war effect, extra timer or world/pair scan is introduced. Native AGOT mechanics,
optional G3 diagnostic and existing AI/player execution rules remain authoritative.

uncertain legacy terms, exact receipt/case identity, immutable copy order,
current-versus-terminal terms, original reader roles, old-data fallback,
read-only navigation, unique writers, clocks/outcomes and EN/FR strings. Full
repository checks and distribution checks also apply. These inspect source wiring;
no Python simulation proves CK3 story persistence, privacy or event semantics.
