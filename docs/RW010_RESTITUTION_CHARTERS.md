# Public institutional restitution charters

**Original CK3 scripts; static-checked, not engine-tested.** This adds an institutional
political undertaking to one exact public land-register entry. It does not migrate
or publish any private restitution promise, create an enforceable land right or
implement G3's universal house landholding ban. Native title decisions stay native.

## Proposal and consent

The current independent king/emperor holding the entry's recorded crown can propose
a public undertaking to pursue return of the **exact title to the recorded former
house when the crown personally holds it**. The current head of that exact house
must explicitly accept within seven days. Both captured representatives must still
occupy those roles at acceptance; no pending personal consent transfers to a new
sovereign or head. Roles must be living/adult/free and distinct. A title already held
by that house cannot start this proposal. No money, prestige, claim or land moves.

Acceptance consumes the seven-day offer window, increments revision, records date
and starts **five years**. It recognizes the issuing sovereign as the initial
responsible ruler. This is an agreed public institutional text, not a personal oath
secretly inherited. One charter proposal is allowed per exact public entry; closure
or expiry never permits restarting the window. Other entries and private discussions
remain independent; this is not a global cap on useful actions or target houses.

## Succession is a choice, not inherited personal guilt

When a different current sovereign reads the active charter, they can explicitly
**take it up**, or **repudiate** it without first promising personally. Taking it up
records the current ruler/date and increments revision before notices, without
changing the accepted deadline. The current house head can release the charter on
behalf of the recorded house. Their role represents the institutional beneficiary;
it never transfers unrelated personal agreement, guilt, opinion or punishment.

Every action rechecks exact public entry/story/serial/crown/house/title identity,
current captured sovereign/head, charter revision/state and window. Old popups cannot
act after role or revision changes. Fresh public navigation captures current roles
and revision; delayed replies never refresh themselves. Existing public reader roles
remain authoritative; a storage custodian receives no new powers simply by owning
the story. Missing roles/references and expired entries fail closed.

Recognition and opportunity coalescing use character identities, not an independently
recorded reign identifier. A last recognized ruler who returns does not automatically
need to recognize again. Unobserved transient role reversals are not tracked, and
no continuous-role-history guarantee is claimed. Current-role guards still apply.

## Recovery is observed ownership plus a statement, not causal proof

Only the current head can explicitly record satisfaction, and only while a living
member of the **exact house currently holds the exact title**. The record captures
the actual holder and acting representative. It does not assert that the crown
granted the title, that the charter caused the recovery, or that a native sanction
was lawful. Recovery by another route is not relabeled as a grant. Nothing auto-
completes on conquest, grant, inheritance, election, title gain or ordinary reading.

No grant/revocation/war/claim/house-ban/native-interaction effect or legal bypass is
added. The charter does not authorize seizing land from a third party. Native title
actions, their AI selection and existing AGOT rules remain separate. This first
slice provides the institutional declaration, representative choices and guarded
recovery acknowledgment, not automated AI execution of a land transfer.

Current representatives may close an unaccepted stale proposal without blame.
Repudiation, release, closure and satisfaction consume windows, increment revision
and capture actual actor/date before notices. Expiry/silence never certifies breach,
fulfillment, guilt or pardon. No opinion penalty or new inherited duty is automatic.

## Storage, opportunities and checks

Charter fields live on the already public indexed land story. Its existing owner-
death/sixty-day storage rehoming carries those fields; it does not copy private
records or change access. The existing fixed hundred-year archive retention is
never extended, including by acceptance near the archive's end. Retention/reference
loss can therefore end access before a charter's nominal five-year period. This is
not a save migration or perpetual archive guarantee. Only the latest recognition
is stored, not an exhaustive history of every reign.

Fresh deposit can offer the issuer an initial charter menu. Older empty entries can
be reviewed manually, but maintenance never invents an automatic new offer for them.
Existing per-entry maintenance offers active-charter opportunities to changed current
representatives, coalesced by last notified identity before sending. Character hidden
events establish the correct recipient root before reader/role checks; invoking a
reader helper directly from a story root is avoided. No new callback, timer, index,
world-pair search or global scan is added. This reuses existing maintenance, not a
claim of immediate succession notification or measured performance.

AI and player use the same proposal/acceptance/recognition/exit/satisfaction actions,
roles and costs. Manual archive navigation is cosmetic. Notice timing, AI choices,
spam, storage survival, scalar snapshots, privacy/save/load and actual title/AGOT
compatibility remain engine-unverified. Durations and weights are provisional.
Nineteen adversarial source tests check identity/current roles, original consent,
revision/clock isolation, succession choices, actual house ownership, terminal actor,
no native/private-state mutation, character-root notices, neutral expiry and EN/FR.
RCHART-01…12 in FINAL_ENGINE_TESTS.md remain unrun for the owner's final campaign.
No new native API, proprietary source, paid service, runtime Python/LLM or intermediate
upload/game run is required. G1 and universal G3 enforcement remain unresolved.
