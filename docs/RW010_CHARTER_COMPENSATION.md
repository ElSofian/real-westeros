# Public compensation in place of an institutional restitution charter

**Original CK3 scripts; static-checked, not engine-tested.** Current representatives
can negotiate a personal gold payment for release of one exact active public crown
restitution charter. This adds structured consideration to an institutional promise;
it creates no house bank balance, native land right or inherited personal debt.

## Explicit terms and counterpart consent

The current sovereign may publicly offer **25 personal gold** to the current head
of the exact recorded house. That head may instead publicly propose release for
**50 personal gold** from the sovereign. The other current representative can accept,
refuse, wait or counter the alternative amount. Each counter increments the financial
revision, swaps responder and captures the latest amount. Proposing/countering
neither pays nor releases the charter. Only the current other party's acceptance of
the exact current amount/version can settle. The sovereign is always payer and the
house head always personal recipient, regardless of which one clicks acceptance.

The financial window is **seven days from proposal**, also constrained by the
charter's original five-year deadline and registry retention. Counter, read, refusal
or waiting renews neither deadline. One dialogue is permitted for the same charter
version and monetary-party tuple. A real representative or charter-version change
can permit a new explicit proposal: it increments the same monotonic financial
revision and freezes the new named pair. This supersedes old unpaid terms without
inheriting anyone's agreement. It is not a renewed charter or full history of every
failed quote. A paid dialogue cannot be overwritten/reopened. Roles are matched by current
character identity, not a separately observed reign epoch: a returning original
representative can still match an unsuperseded unexpired quote. Unobserved transient
role reversals are not a promised invalidation signal.

Every response rechecks exact public entry/story/crown/house/title, current charter
revision, financial revision/amount, frozen monetary parties, current representative
roles, active state and both windows. A successor, changed head, new recognition,
expired/renounced/released/satisfied charter or stale popup cannot spend an old
quote. The named sovereign must still have the agreed **personal gold** at the
moment of acceptance. No escrow, credit, negative-balance fallback, house treasury,
AGOT debt inheritance or automatic borrowing is implemented.

## Paired consumption, then the native exchange

Settlement first consumes the financial window/turn, records agreed amount/revision/
date and actual accepting actor, then consumes/releases the exact charter and
increments its revision with the same actor/date. It captures the resulting charter
revision before sovereign debit, head credit and only then private-to-representative
notices of the public agreement. No generic charter-close helper is called before
gold, because its immediate notices would precede the exchange. Tests keep the
release bookkeeping aligned with the existing free-release shape.

The receipt binds original monetary parties and agreed quote to the resulting
released-charter revision, actual actor and date. Both financial and general charter
views require complete receipt metadata before describing a paid release. Later
current rulers/heads can read the public historical receipt but are not substituted
as payer/recipient. These ordered native effects are **not an atomic engine
transaction or crash-recovery guarantee**; no retry of a partial exchange is added.

## Freedom and public memory

Either current representative may close an unpaid stale offer freely; that changes
only the dialogue. Existing free charter repudiation, head release or actual-house-
ownership satisfaction remain available, including from the financial panel. Their
normal parent revision/state change immediately invalidates old financial acceptance.
No refusal/expiry creates resentment, a new debt, payment or charter release.

Financial terms are explicitly public under the land registry's existing reader
policy. Original named monetary parties are shown separately from later office
holders. Explicit archive navigation captures current financial metadata only;
delayed replies never refresh their own consent or the parent charter snapshot.
Missing/old/unknown metadata fails closed. The slot retains the latest unpaid terms
or terminal paid receipt, not a perpetual ledger. Original charter facts, other
promises/cases, archive retention, custody migration, legal facts, title ownership,
claims, opinions, native war and AGOT banking stay unchanged. Paid release is not
recovery of land, a pardon, restored noble status or a universal G3 title-ban remedy.

AI and player share exact actor/current-role/funds/version guards, prices and effects.
Existing charter notices offer either proposal; one-day counterpart notices provide
bounded replies until the original window ends. No new native callback, periodic
notification, global scan, index or runtime Python/LLM is added. Weights/balance and
actual AI behavior/privacy/save compatibility are unmeasured.

Nineteen adversarial source tests cover current representatives, role/version
succession, price/responder/counter guards, current personal funds, paired terminal
consumption before transfer/notices, composite receipt integrity, no replay/deadline
reset, free exits, public historical names, unchanged native/private state and EN/FR.
These are not a CK3 simulation. CCOMP-01…12 in FINAL_ENGINE_TESTS.md remain unrun;
scalar snapshots, root/role/date transitions, actual transfers, partial-effect
ordering, access/storage/save/load/AI/economy/performance and AGOT compatibility need
the final campaign. Existing gold APIs are reused; no proprietary source, new paid
service, additional upload or intermediate game run is required. G1 and universal
G3 enforcement remain separate/unresolved. Next wider institutional clauses and
contextual political consequences across all eight systems.
